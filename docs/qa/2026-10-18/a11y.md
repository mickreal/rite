# Accessibility pass (WCAG 2.1 AA), 18 Oct 2026

Companion to `report.md`. Contrast computed from rendered pages (48 unique text/background pairs) by `tools/table.mjs`.

## Fixed

- **Focus:** `:focus-visible` gets a 2px #F2B544 ring with 3px offset (#14110A on cream surfaces, `.cream`); `.inp` and
  `.ta` get the same focus glow as `.field` inputs.
- **Contrast nudges (look kept):**
  - #6B675F → #8A857B (3.0–3.4:1 → 4.6–5.2:1): hints, TOC counts, chart and scrubber labels, placeholders.
  - Cream ink at .5/.55 alpha → .62 (4.89:1): mini-note labels, `/season` paper footer.
  - `/gift` ◆ bullets (2.60:1): now `aria-hidden`, #8A6A1E.
  - Past tiles and locked workbook card opacity .72/.7 → .82; "Look back" badge → #BDB6A8 (5.66:1).
- **ARIA:** scrubber `aria-valuetext` follows the month shown (tested: two ArrowRights → "Dec 2026"); `aria-pressed` on
  pass pills, ages, date chips, year tabs; live regions on narration status, Ask error, story message and `/start`
  errors; `aria-controls` on FAQ toggles (already had `aria-expanded`); `autocomplete` on email and first name.
- **Reduced motion:** theme-wide `*{animation:none}` plus a fix for every `opacity:0` start state; JS count-up and both
  smooth scrolls respect the setting. Automated check: no text or SVG left at opacity 0 on any page.

**Already fine:** all 46 SVGs `aria-hidden` or labelled (hero orbit is `role="img"` with a label); all inputs labelled;
one h1 and a title per page; Tab walk reaches every control with a visible focus change; keyboard path through `/start`
and the reading (TOC, play, scrubber with arrows, chips, tabs, workbook, arc tiles) works.
