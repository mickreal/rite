What it tends to be about

Aries is the sign of starting things, acting on your own and going first. Saturn there asks for the slower version of that: leadership that holds up over time, and independence you can actually support.

People in this return often describe questions about self-direction. Am I building my own thing or someone else's? What am I willing to be responsible for?

Why it lasts years, not days

Saturn takes about 29 years to circle the zodiac and moves slowly enough to cross the same degree up to three times as it goes retrograde and direct again. Your exact passes depend on the degree Saturn held on the day you were born, which is why two people born a year apart get different dates.
