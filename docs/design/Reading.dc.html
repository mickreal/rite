Chapter one

Your Saturn

Saturn was at 20° Aries when you were born. Aries starts things, acts alone and goes first. Saturn is the planet of structure, time and consequences. Put together, your Saturn asks you to learn the slow version of courage: leadership that holds up after the first burst of energy runs out.

Earlier in life this often shows up as either holding back from starting things, or starting a lot and finishing little. The return is when Saturn comes back to check which pattern you have built.

In your ninth house

With a Leo rising, Saturn sits in your ninth house: beliefs, study, travel, publishing and the big questions of what you think life is for. Your return is likely to press hardest on what you believe and where you are heading, more than on day-to-day work.
